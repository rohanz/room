## Findings

- **F1 (medium) = P2, still present in 0.17.** `wake-reconciler.ts` (`wakeable()`/`pass()`, lines 90–148) wakes
  a Codex session through `codex queue` for each new message even while that session is mid-turn. The before-tool
  hook then delivers the same message inline, and the queued turn replays it once the turn ends ("Call
  room_state; it shows them" — and room_state shows nothing new). Werkzeug W3 is the same mechanism for a push.
  Suspected fix: do not queue a Codex wake while the session has had hook contact in the last few seconds; wake
  only once it is quiet, and re-check the ledger before sending.
- **F2 (low).** After a turn the human interrupted (Esc), `codex queue` wakes did not start a turn, though Room
  logged "woke". An interrupted Codex session is effectively unwakeable until its human types.
- **F3 (low).** ana's presence read "synced with origin/r17-flask" while `origin/r17-flask` was one commit ahead
  (HEAD and the local `origin/` ref were `2ff8ee9`, the remote `888623b`). Room had fetched ben's commit by SHA and
  knew of the push, but the status compares against the stale tracking ref.
- **F4 (low).** An Esc during `room_claim` left the claim in place while the host reported "Failed … interrupted".
- **F5 (low, design).** cy's offline claim and scope outlived the work: ben finished the same refactor and ana
  landed cy's files, but `c_mun473t7abj1a6` stayed and Room "will keep flagging a conflict" until expiry. There
  is no way for a teammate to release a departed participant's claim.
- **F6 (low).** Codex MCPs log `stopping: SIGTERM` and nothing else (no "name lease … ended", no "stopped");
  Claude MCPs log all three. Presence then ends by awareness timeout (~30 s) rather than at once. Also, logs print
  Codex thread ids truncated to 8 characters, which for UUIDv7 ids started together are identical
  (`01a0eec4` for both ben and cy).
- **F7 (wording).** ben's preview said "included cy at 2b1a766fa4 (pushed to origin/r17-flask)", although cy had
  never pushed; `2b1a766` was the run's starting commit.
