# Soak test 2: Room 0.17.0-rc4 on staging, 2026-10-01

The [first soak](2026-10-01-soak.md) (rc3) found CPU that grew with the bus until shared-cpu-1x throttled,
native memory growth, and idle memory held by the startup inventory scan. rc4 (`redesign` at `3e26a19`, tag
`v0.17.0-rc4`) adds incremental bus indexes with batched trims, removes the startup scan, runs a compiled server
under plain node with `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`, and fixes the volume-less logout and the in-flight post on close.
This is the same 3-hour run with the same harness and load, rerun against rc4.

## Setup

Identical to soak 1 except:
- **Server.** New staging machine `2863604c406678` (sin, shared-cpu-1x, 512 MB) on a fresh empty volume, image
  `rc4`. The process is `node packages/server/dist/server.mjs` with `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`; there is no tsx
  wrapper or esbuild process.
- **Harness.** [`scripts/soak.mts`](../../../scripts/soak.mts):
  - matches the rc4 process;
  - records machine-wide steal from `/proc/stat` (steal % is the share of all CPU ticks in the interval that
    the host withheld);
  - polls Fly logs every minute itself;
  - treats a stall window as 3 or more health failures within 5 minutes;
  - discounts a pid reused across the restart.
  Soak 1's numbers below are recomputed from its saved events with the same analysis.
- **Timing.** Start 04:45:34Z, restart at minute 90 (06:15:40Z), soak end 07:45:34Z, idle samples to 07:52:50Z.

## Findings

1. **A lease can lapse on a connected client after a short stall (should fix; does not block release).**
   - **What happened.** At 06:41:27–06:41:43Z (minute 116) requests on several websockets stalled for about
     16 s. Posts answered after 2.5–20.6 s, one probe got `fetch failed`, and soak-f's socket was replaced. A
     network or Fly-edge blip is likely; the server's own health check never failed.
   - **The loss.** soak-g's and soak-h's last acknowledged renew was at 06:41:09. Hub and clients both expired
     the leases at 06:41:54–55, which is 45 s later, although the sockets had worked again for 10 s by then.
   - **Why.** The client renews only on its 15 s tick (`packages/room-mcp/src/hub-client.ts:104`). A renew that
     times out (10 s) is not retried before the next tick. After a reconnect, `handshake()` renews only when the
     incarnation changed (`packages/room-mcp/src/hub-client.ts:173`). So a stall that spans two ticks costs the
     lease even when the connection is back before the TTL.
   - **Effect.** Coordination paused for about 1 s, then the same names were re-acquired under new epochs. No
     message was lost, no name drifted, and fencing held.

   **Correction (2026-10-02).** The first version of this report checked a disconnect's epoch at the first status
   after reconnecting, before a lapse could show, and so under-counted. Rechecked 60 s later:
   - The same missing renew after a reconnect also cost 3 leases 6–15 s after short disconnects: soak-a at minutes
     85 and 150, soak-h at minute 28.
   - So connected losses total 5, and short disconnects (under 30 s) kept their epoch in 24/30 cases, not 30/30.
   - Another 9 leases were found expired within 5 s of reconnecting from a disconnect of 30 s or more. Their
     age (the disconnect plus up to 15 s since the last renew) had usually passed the 45 s TTL, so those are
     expected.
   - Recomputed with the same rule, soak 1 had 40 connected losses, most of them in its throttled window.

   rc5 renews right after every hello, which addresses both causes.
   - **Fix.** Renew right after any successful hello, and retry a failed renew with a short backoff while the
     lease is still valid.
2. **Memory is bounded but not flat.**
   - **During the run.** RSS rises in the first hour as both rooms' documents and buses fill (94 → 150 MB), then
     moves between 142 and 166 MB until the end. Over 180 minutes the HWM was 202 MB against 309 MB in soak 1.
     The 15%-growth criterion fails because the first hour includes that fill.
   - **After the run.** Idle RSS settled at 144.5 MB, not the 73.6 MB baseline: the observer still holds both
     rooms loaded (about 5.5 MB of documents).
   - **Verdict.** Comfortable on 512 MB for this load; not release-blocking. A longer soak would show whether
     the post-restart drift (148 → 166 → 155 MB) is a plateau.
3. **Fixed since soak 1, confirmed here.**
   - CPU no longer scales with the bus: 1.8% at 3,000 entries against 10.9% at 2,650.
   - No throttling: steal stayed at or below 0.3%, against 32% in soak 1.
   - Idle RSS is 73.6 MB against 100 MB (124 MB with the tsx wrapper).
   - No health stalls.
   - Restart reconnection is faster.
   - The no-persistence logout and in-flight-on-close bugs were not exercised by staging; the lead verified them
     locally.

No release-blocking bug was found in rc4.
