## Findings

- **W1 (medium, host).** Claude Code's auto-mode classifier denied ana's first `room_join(where="team")` with no
  reason. The same call from ben passed. With terse humans this cost a round trip and a "yes" the human could not
  have reasoned about. Suspected cause: the classifier treats a team join as sending repository content to an
  external server; the tool description's disclosure wording may be what trips it.
- **W2 (low).** `room_create` took 6.3 s, 4.2 s of it in the `name` phase, on an empty local server.
- **W3 (low).** A push wake reached cy 27 s after it had fetched and rebased onto that same push; the wake is
  not dropped when the notice's head is already in the clone.
- **W4 (low, wording).** ana's inbox at 19:38:36.291Z held `POSSIBLE conflict cleared on tests/test_wrappers.py`
  twice, and each line repeats the path: "POSSIBLE conflict cleared on tests/test_wrappers.py:
  tests/test_wrappers.py: the possible conflict with cy cleared".

## What worked

- Three one-line tasks produced three correct fixes without a single coordination prompt from a human.
- Two rejected pushes both stopped and asked; nothing was rebased without a yes; the branch history is linear.
- Presence ended within seconds of every quit.
