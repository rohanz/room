## (b) Diverged branch plus a force-push reset: the resolver keeps working, the pair says "cannot compare" — PASS

`diverge.sh` builds the following:
- A pushes `a1`. B commits `b1` on the old tip and fetches, so B is diverged: ahead 1, behind 1.
- A resets to `c0`, commits `a2` and force-pushes, so `a1` is on no ref.
- C is a `--no-local` `file://` clone that never saw `a1`.

`diverge.mts` runs `roomRemote` → `readBaseRefs` → `resolveBase` in each clone. It then runs `comparePair` over
every pair, plus A's stale pre-force-push record `{base: a1}`, which is still in the room until A republishes.

## (c) H1: an app-server session idle 8 h releases its claims and scope, with one notice — PASS

`idle.mts` sets up a real `RoomDoc`, the wave-1 `WorkerRegistry`, and an injected monotonic clock (`clock`, in
ms of the session's own idle time):
- `ben` (`holder.sessionId = s-ben`, host `shared-app-server`) holds two claims and a scope.
- `ann` holds one claim.
- `postNotice` posts through `doc.post(…, {id})`.

```
npx tsx idle.mts /tmp/room-w1-a/.git

```

```
idle 8h00m: released=true benClaims=0 annClaims=1 benScope=false notices=1
idle 9h (repeat reconcile): released=false benClaims=0 annClaims=1 benScope=false notices=1

notice idle-claims:s-ben:idle-1: ben released claims src/api.ts:2-5, src/db.ts:10-12 and cleared its scope after 8 h idle
```

- At 7 h 59 m nothing happened. At 8 h ben's claims and scope were released, with exactly one notice.
- The notice has the deterministic id, names both claims, and leaves ann's claim alone.
- Neither a repeated reconcile nor a reopened store posted a second notice.
- An interactive CLI session is never released for being quiet.
- Clearing the scope is a lead extension of H1 (registry §18, open question 5). Without it, a scope-only session
  would never reach the 30-minute idle lease.
- Other cases are in `worker-registry.test.ts`:
  - claims-only wording after review S7;
  - a successor holder under the same name is not released;
  - crash replay after the post.
