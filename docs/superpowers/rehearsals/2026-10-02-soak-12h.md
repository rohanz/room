# 12-hour soak: Room 0.17.0-rc5 on staging, 2026-10-01/02

This run settles the open question from [soak 2](2026-10-01-soak-rc4.md): is the server's memory a plateau or a
slow drift? It also checks rc5's lease fix. rc5 is `redesign` at `08f6a5a` (tag `v0.17.0-rc5`): the hub client
renews right after every hello and retries a failed renew at 1/2/4 s within the validity window.

**Answer: a slow drift. It is not a process leak, and a restart does not reset it.** RSS rises about
7 MB/hour under this load and is still rising after 12 hours. Its source is Yjs history that is never removed:
every post, receipt, claim and overlay edit leaves structs (deleted items included) in the room document, and
those survive every trim. CPU drifts the same way, because the hub's per-minute trim walks maps that keep their
tombstones. Leases, delivery and counters pass across both restarts.

## Setup

Same harness ([`scripts/soak.mts`](../../../scripts/soak.mts)) and load as soaks 1 and 2. Eight participants
(fake logins `soak-a` … `soak-h`) in `github.com/soak/alpha` (5) and `github.com/soak/beta` (3) across 3 branches:
- a message every 30–90 s each, half of them addressed questions;
- claims on shared files, and edits including 100–500 KB files;
- a disconnect every 10–20 min lasting 5–75 s;
- one leave-and-rejoin each, spread over the 12 h.

What differs from soak 2:
- **Server.** New staging machine `2879590f442648` (sin, shared-cpu-1x, 512 MB), fresh empty volume, image
  rc5 (`node packages/server/dist/server.mjs`, `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`).
- **Timing.** 720 minutes from 2026-10-01 08:27:36Z, with restarts at minutes 180 (11:27:45Z) and 480
  (16:27:44Z). Participants stopped at 20:27:44Z, followed by 6 min of idle samples.
- **Report changes in `soak.mts`.**
  - A list of restarts.
  - A least-squares RSS line fitted from hour 1, leaving out the 10 min after each restart, plus a CPU line.
  - An hourly table.
  - A connected-lease-loss check: a pause that begins while the client's socket and hub are up, outside a
    leave. It is separated from a lease found already expired within 5 s of reconnecting from a disconnect of
    30 s or more.
  - Duplicates classified as at-least-once when the first receipt was written offline and its replica was
    discarded by a leave before reconnecting.
  - A `structs` mode that counts each room document's Yjs structs.
  - Incarnations taken from the hub's `meta` mirror, which rc5's client no longer exposes.

## Memory: slope

| Window (restart + 10 min excluded) | Slope | r |
|---|---|---|
| Hours 1–12 (the criterion) | **7.07 MB/hour** (intercept 131 MB, 62 samples) | 0.90 |
| Hours 1–3 (fill: buses and documents growing) | 14.5 MB/hour | 0.88 |
| Hours 3–8 (between the restarts) | 6.6 MB/hour | 0.66 |
| Hours 6–12 (buses already at their cap) | 6.4 MB/hour | 0.71 |
| Hours 8–12 (after restart 2) | 8.1 MB/hour | 0.60 |

- **Restarts do not reset it.** RSS just after a restart was 149.6 MB at minute 180 and 200.2 MB at minute
  480. A freshly loaded server holds more as the stored history grows. The idle RSS after the run was 198 MB,
  with nobody connected.
- **Peaks.** Maximum RSS was 225.9 MB and HWM 285.2 MB, so under the 300 MB limit, but the line reaches 300 MB
  at about 24 h of this load.
- **The documents' history grows linearly.** Struct counts, from read-only syncs with `soak.mts structs`:

| Sample | alpha structs (deleted items) | beta structs (deleted items) | alpha map keys `archive` / `mail` / `claims` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minute 482 | 55,922 (39,185) | 29,939 (20,277) | 4,031 / 453 / 695 |
| Minute 688 | 79,918 (58,251) | 44,284 (30,981) | 6,616 / 758 / 990 |
| After the stop | 83,670 (61,673) | 44,834 (31,135) | — |

  That is about 11,200 structs per hour, roughly 16 per post at this load. They don't shrink when everyone
  leaves; only the encoded size drops, as overlays are withdrawn.
- **What the structs are.**
  - Distinct new keys in per-message maps account for about 20%: `archive` (a key per archived message; its
    5,000-entry retention cap bounds the live entries, but the 6,616 keys include the tombstones of evicted
    ones), `seen:<P>` receipts, `mail`, `claims` and outcomes.
  - Most of the rest are overwrites of hot keys. Yjs keeps a deleted Item for each overwritten value it cannot
    merge (for example `meta.hubSeq`, written with every bus append), and trimmed bus items are kept as deleted
    structs too.
  - Measured locally, one such tombstone struct costs about 305 B of V8 heap. 11,200/hour × 305 B is about
    3.4 MB/hour of heap. The observed 7 MB/hour of RSS fits that plus V8 headroom; not separated on staging.
- **CPU follows the same history.** Y.Map iteration visits every key ever set and filters out deleted ones
  (`createMapIterator`, `node_modules/yjs/dist/yjs.mjs:5704`). The hub's 60 s maintenance `trim()`
  (`packages/hub-core/src/hub.ts:727`) iterates `mail`, `archive` and `outcomes`, so its cost grows with their
  tombstones. CPU went from 1.1% to 3.8% of a core (hourly averages), a fit of +0.20 points/hour, while the bus
  was capped. Steal stayed at or below 1.3%, with no throttling.

## Findings

1. **Room documents keep their whole Yjs history.**
   - **Release impact.** Not blocking a short trial at real rates. It does block indefinitely long-lived busy
     rooms on 512 MB.
   - **What grows.** Every operation adds structs that trims never remove: about 16 per post here. Memory and
     CPU grow linearly with total traffic, and so does the cost of loading a room after a restart.
   - **How big.** This load (about 700 posts/hour across 8 agents) is far above real agent traffic. At a tenth
     of it the drift is about 0.7 MB/hour, or 17 MB/day, still unbounded over weeks.
   - **Fix.** Compact the document: rebuild it from its current state into a fresh Y.Doc. Because live client
     replicas would re-merge the old history, this needs a document generation that clients check before they
     sync (drop the replica and resync). For example, compact at load when the struct count exceeds about
     10× the live entries, and bump the generation.
   - **Cheaper partial step.** Stop overwriting hot keys on every post: write `meta.hubSeq` and the others
     less often, or derive them.
2. **The rc5 lease fix works.** No connected client lost its lease in 12 hours, 366 disconnects and 2
   restarts (rc4 lost 5 in 3 hours).
3. **Harness note.** A leave during the simulated disconnect left the disconnect loop calling a cleared
   session (one `loop-error` at minute 276). It had no effect on the run; it is guarded now.
