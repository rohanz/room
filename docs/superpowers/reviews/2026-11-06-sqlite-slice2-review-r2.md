1. **MUST-FIX — Lazy scopes allow lifecycle-derived effects before acquiring their ticket.** `packages/room-mcp/src/storage-admission.ts:205` installs an ambient scope but does not admit it. `withValidatedRead` (`:173`) records a generation, and `acquire` (`:64`) correctly checks that generation, including the initialization-only 0→1 exception. However, that fence is useful only if an effect actually calls an acquiring API first. Several production paths use validated lifecycle facts to perform effects without doing so:

   - `packages/room-mcp/src/bridge.ts:648` reads a worker snapshot, selects targets at `:652`, and posts to them at `:669`. `relayDown` has no acquiring call. Maintenance can drain after the snapshot and before delivery, observe zero tickets, and complete while that lifecycle-derived message is still in flight.
   - `packages/room-mcp/src/bridge.ts:248` reads the snapshot and calls `syncCoordination` at `:254`, before the subsequent projection's admission. `syncCoordination` changes CRDT coordination using worker facts at `:268`–`:277`. Initial attachment and synchronous claim mirroring also consume the cached worker facts (`:199`, `:591`); acquiring later in projection cannot undo an earlier coordination effect.
   - `packages/room-mcp/src/tools/messaging.ts:196` decides whether to resume from worker status. The already-running branch reaches `post()` at `:301`–`:304` without acquisition when publication is already current or unnecessary. `retryPublication` itself starts publication at `:238` before the registry command at `:239` acquires; its catch at `:241` explicitly continues message delivery after failure.
   - `packages/room-mcp/src/tools/workers.ts:102` decides which mirrored claims to retain from running-worker facts. For a nonworker caller, `:143` releases claims and `:144` posts without acquiring. For a worker caller, publication at `:116` starts before `reportDone` acquires at `:118`.

   A no-file, dependency-injected probe using the current transpiled `Bridge.relayDown` and admission implementation suspended `local.post`: it entered with zero tickets; simulated drain completed with zero tickets; delivery resumed in `draining` with zero tickets. This is a reproducer of the missing admission boundary, not a native end-to-end maintenance run. The owning lead confirmed that lifecycle-derived policy, CRDT, Git and process effects must acquire first; there is no intended earlier fence in these paths.

   Acquire once before the first consequential use of lifecycle facts, preserving the observed-generation check, and retain that ticket through the final effect. Audit leaf helpers as well: `retire.ts:28`, `retire.ts:74` and `worker-git.ts:481` rely on an already-admitted caller before policy/process/Git effects; their current collect/sweep/replay parents provide protection, but a lazy wrapper alone does not establish it. Add focused boundary regressions that pause before the first effect and prove drain cannot complete. Violated rule: M2's compatibility admission contract, the spec's prohibition on display snapshots granting effect authority, and `docs/reference.md:208`'s ticket-through-effects guarantee.

2. **MUST-FIX — Publication timeout returns while its effect continues beyond ticket completion.** `packages/room-mcp/src/worker-publication.ts:15` races reconciliation/settlement against a five-second timeout and catches the timeout as a completed best-effort call. It neither cancels nor waits for the underlying async branch. `packages/room-mcp/src/kept-publication.ts:46` does the same: Git/disk work may remain pending before reconciliation at `:69`, while the timer at `:120` returns a pending acknowledgement to the caller. The new admission wrapper finishes the operation's ticket as soon as its callback returns (`packages/room-mcp/src/storage-admission.ts:151`, `:211`). An inherited AsyncLocalStorage context does not retain a live ticket after that finish.

   Concrete scenario: a collect or completion call holds admission, publication stalls, its timeout returns, and the caller records the pending result or completion and returns. Drain now sees no ticket. The delayed branch subsequently enters or completes daemon reconciliation/publication, and may launch additional Git reads, after the caller has relinquished admission. This remains a bug even after finding 1's missing initial acquisition is fixed. A no-file probe using the actual transpiled publication/admission functions, a suspended reconciliation dependency and an accelerated timeout observed `tickets=0` when the outer operation returned and again when the delayed publication effect completed.

   Retain independently tracked admission until the underlying work actually finishes, or implement cancellation/fencing that prevents further effects and await its completion before finishing the ticket. Merely racing the returned promise or marking acknowledgement pending is insufficient. Violated rule: M2 requires the ticket through effects and acknowledgements, including independently running callbacks/helpers; `packages/local-state/src/protocol.ts:202` and `:212` make detached lifetime and drain completion explicit. These existing publication helpers need integration with the new lifetime contract.
