1. **MUST-FIX — Automatic unregister defeats the live-holder lost-ticket safeguard.** `packages/room-mcp/src/storage-admission.ts:134`–`:148` periodically unregisters every draining registration, without consulting an in-memory inventory of unfinished operations or helpers. `packages/local-state/src/gate.ts:341`–`:347` allows unregister whenever the ticket directory has no matching files. The durable marker therefore does not protect a live MCP's work when its volatile ticket disappears.

   Concrete interleaving: an MCP enters `withAdmission`, obtains its ticket, and pauses inside an asynchronous Git/publication effect (`storage-admission.ts:174`–`:181`). Simulate loss of that ticket file while keeping the MCP and effect alive, as the accepted cheap-ticket contract expressly requires. Maintenance publishes draining. Initially `registrationEvidence` correctly reports the live marked holder (`gate.ts:395`–`:397`). The one-second MCP sweep then deletes its registration because the lost ticket is absent. The next drain sees no names or registration evidence and returns complete (`gate.ts:404`–`:425`), while the suspended operation can resume and acknowledge its effect. No parent callback has finished. A lost helper ticket can suffer the same premature acknowledgement while its MCP remains alive.

   This is a source-established interleaving, not a claim that machine power loss leaves its processes running; no injected reproduction of this integration scenario was run. It violates the explicit live/paused-holder ticket-loss guarantee in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-sqlite-state.md:152` and the whole-operation barrier at `:146`. Track locally outstanding admissions (including acquisition in progress and detached/helper lifetime) independently of ticket files, and permit clean automatic unregister only after those lifetimes acknowledge completion. Add an integration regression that loses a ticket during a paused effect, advances the sweep, and proves drain remains incomplete until actual completion. The isolated gate's conservative marker tests do not cover the MCP deleting that marker.
